# Omo Beeza Ministry — Content Reference

Canonical copy for the site. The live source of truth is `messages/en.json` (English)
and `messages/am.json` (Amharic). This document mirrors the key brand content for easy
review. **Tone:** Christian missional — keep gospel-centered language ("Christ-like",
"the Gospel", "the Word of God", "discipleship", "prayer"). The name **"Beeza"** is
Amharic for **"redemption."**

## Vision (chosen: Option 1)
> To raise a generation in South Omo, discipled within their own context and holistically
> developed to serve as Christ-like, socially responsible agents of change in their
> communities.

## Mission (chosen: Option 1)
Equip the children of South Omo through holistic education and spiritual discipleship by:
- **Enlightening** them with scriptural truth, academic excellence, and vocational skills.
- **Fostering** a Biblical worldview and a passion for the Great Commission.
- **Raising up** the next generation of transformational leaders for the Church and society.

## Values (About page)
- **Compassion** — unconditional love modeled after Christ.
- **Faith** — Scripture-led, prayer-dependent in all we do.
- **Family** — genuine homes where children belong and thrive.
- **Integrity** — transparency and accountability to supporters.

## Programs / What We Do
- **Education** — schooling, academic excellence, vocational skills.
- **Nutrition & Care** — shelter, food, medical care, daily wellbeing.
- **Joy & Community** — discipleship, belonging, play, spiritual growth.
