Every child who comes through our doors is a gift and a calling.

This season, we joyfully welcomed a new child into the Omo Beeza family. We believe the
Lord sent this little one to us — both to protect a precious life and to bless our home
with new joy.

## What it means to belong

At Omo Beeza, belonging is not a program — it is a family. Each child receives:

- A safe and loving home
- Daily prayer, worship, and discipleship
- Education, nutrition, and medical care

Thank you for your prayers and support that make moments like these possible. Please keep
our growing family in your prayers as we raise each child in the love of Christ.
