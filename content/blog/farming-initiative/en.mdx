We praise God for a season of growth — both in the soil and in the lives of our children.

On the 0.7 hectares of land entrusted to us, believers from the local community came
together to plough and sow corn. What began as bare ground is now a living picture of
God's faithfulness and the dignity of work.

## Why farming matters

Our vision is **holistic** — caring for the whole child and the whole community. The farm:

- Helps feed the children in our care
- Teaches vocational and life skills rooted in stewardship
- Builds partnership and trust with the surrounding community

> "Let us not grow weary of doing good, for in due season we will reap, if we do not give up." — Galatians 6:9

Please continue to pray for **rain in due season** and for the Lord's continued provision
over the harvest.
